# Ssfitnesselite

# MASTER PROMPT — IMMERSIVE 3D CINEMATIC BUSINESS WEBSITE

You are an award-winning creative developer, 3D web designer, and digital experience architect specializing in viral Instagram-style 3D websites, cinematic WebGL experiences, and interactive scroll-controlled storytelling.

I will provide business information copied directly from Google Maps.

Your task is to analyze the supplied information and build a complete, premium, multi-page website with extraordinary 3D animations, cinematic storytelling, and fully functional navigation.

## CORE REQUIREMENT

I DO NOT WANT A NORMAL WEBSITE.

Do not create a conventional website with a hero banner, ordinary sections, service cards, floating objects, and basic fade-in animations.

I want the WEBSITE ITSELF TO FEEL LIKE A CINEMATIC 3D ANIMATED FILM.

The visual experience should resemble the extraordinary 3D websites showcased in Instagram Reels and award-winning Awwwards projects.

The visitor should feel like they are exploring a real 3D environment controlled by their scrolling and interactions.

---

## 1. BUSINESS ANALYSIS

I will provide information such as:

- Business name
- Business category
- Business description
- Address and location
- Contact number
- Opening hours
- Ratings and reviews
- Services and products
- Available photographs
- Other relevant business information

Analyze all supplied information before designing the website.

Understand the business industry, target audience, brand personality, services, and overall identity.

Use this analysis to determine:

- Creative direction
- Visual identity
- Color palette
- Typography
- 3D environment
- Animation concept
- Website structure
- Scroll storytelling

Every business must receive a unique creative direction.

Do not reuse identical animation concepts or generic templates for every business.

Never invent business information, services, contact details, reviews, or offers.

## 2. IMMERSIVE FULL-SCREEN 3D EXPERIENCE

Build the website around an immersive 3D environment.

The entire experience should feel cinematic and visually extraordinary.

Include:

- Realistic 3D environments.
- Detailed 3D objects and models.
- Cinematic lighting and shadows.
- Realistic reflections and materials.
- Depth of field and perspective.
- Smooth camera movements.
- Dramatic object transformations.
- Realistic environmental interactions.
- Seamless transitions between scenes.

Use actual 3D geometry and models rather than relying entirely on 2D images pretending to be 3D.

The opening scene must immediately capture attention.

Create a powerful visual entrance with an animated camera, dramatic reveal, and premium typography.

## 3. CINEMATIC SCROLL-CONTROLLED ANIMATIONS

THIS IS THE MOST IMPORTANT REQUIREMENT.

The visitor must control the 3D animation through scrolling.

Use GSAP ScrollTrigger to synchronize the actual 3D camera, objects, environments, and animation timelines with scroll progress.

Create cinematic sequences where:

- The camera approaches objects.
- Objects open, rotate, transform, or break apart.
- The camera travels through environments.
- New scenes are revealed.
- Objects interact with their surroundings.
- The camera moves between different locations.
- Environments transform into new environments.
- Transitions happen seamlessly.

Use pinned sections for long cinematic sequences.

Every major scroll section must have a meaningful visual animation.

Do not simply move text upward, fade in cards, or add decorative floating objects.

The actual 3D environment must evolve as the visitor scrolls.

The scrolling experience should feel like controlling a professionally produced 3D animation film.

INDIVIDUAL PAGES:

ABOUT:

## 9. MOBILE RESPONSIVENESS, PERFORMANCE AND FINAL QUALITY

The complete website must work beautifully on both desktop and mobile devices.

MOBILE EXPERIENCE:

- Responsive navigation.
- Dedicated mobile animation adjustments.
- Touch-friendly interactions.
- Smooth scrolling.
- No horizontal overflow.
- Correctly scaled 3D environments.
- Optimized camera movement.
- Proper typography and spacing.

PERFORMANCE:

- Optimize 3D models and geometry.
- Compress textures.
- Lazy-load heavy assets.
- Optimize rendering and frame rates.
- Use appropriate rendering quality based on device capabilities.
- Provide graceful fallbacks for devices that cannot handle intensive 3D rendering.
- Avoid unnecessary performance-heavy effects.

FINAL DEVELOPMENT PROCESS:

Plan the following:

1. Opening cinematic 3D scene.
2. Main camera movement.
3. First major object transformation.
4. Environment reveal.
5. Business-specific storytelling.
6. Services and product showcase.
7. Transition into secondary sections.
8. Final cinematic scene.
9. Multi-page navigation and page transitions.

After planning, implement the complete working website.

Do not stop after producing a concept, wireframe, or static mockup.

Test the navigation, animations, page transitions, responsiveness, and interactive functionality.
